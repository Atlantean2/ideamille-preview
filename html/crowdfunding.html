IdeaMille

mille - French for 1000 - a thousand good ideas are needed to make an ecosystem of excellent entrepreneurial successes happen

mill - you may have your seed, but until the mill processes it into flour and bakes it into bread ... what have you really got?

MISSION: 100 of 1000

The world needs better ideas, but even moreso, it needs TEAMS that make more good ideas happen. It’s not just a matter of sorting to get only the best ideas, but of DOING more of the great ideas that exist, to build the next “insanely great” ECOSYSTEM of many high quality products and real market businesses. Not just 1 out of a 1000 nor 10 out of a 1000, but 100 of every 1,000.

This site will sift and mill the seeds of ideas in teams of entrepreneurs and helpers. With the addition of bright and synergistic ideas throughout the local community, and with many  entrepreneurs involved, we can transform ideas into the future companies that will become the lifeblood of jobs and capitalism

Ideas sometimes start out local, entrepreneurs are local, and angel investors are local, but businesses can and must function with excellence anywhere and everywhere. We have all the tools and all the experience to help you make your excellence happen.

Michael Hentschel   TechVest Ventures
