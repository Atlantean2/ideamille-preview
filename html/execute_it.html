The world needs better ideas, but even moreso, it needs TEAMS that make more good ideas happen. It’s not just a matter of sorting to get only the best ideas, but of DOING more of the great ideas that exist, to build the next “insanely great” ECOSYSTEM of many high quality products and real market businesses. Not just 1 out of a 1000 nor 10 out of a 1000, but 100 of every 1,000.

This site will sift and mill the seeds of ideas in teams of entrepreneurs and helpers. This checklist (by DocStoc and available downloadablehere) breaks down everything you must and should do when starting a business (archived).

Mandatory tasks are required under the law in order to operate a valid and compliant business.

Recommended tasks, although not strictly required under the law, are tasks that should be undertaken in order to grow and protect a new business.

.Building a Team

Mandatory

o Determine the company founders and leadership (partnership (archived), owners, CEO, etc.)

o Clarify who gets equity, and how much

o Conduct job interviews (archived)—look out for red flags

Recommended

o Find team members through personal connections, recruiters or job boards (Ziprecruiter, Craigslist, Monster,Indeed, SimplyHired)

o Run a background check (archived) on new hires

o For one-off or task based activities, use AmazonTurk or Odesk

.

Human Resources

Mandatory

o Make sure workers are properly classified (employee, independent contractor, part-time employee (archived), intern, etc.)

o Have employees fill out Form I-9 and Form W-4

o Familiarize yourself with the Department of Labor laws and regulations regarding employment

o Put up the necessary labor law posters to meet federal and state requirements

o Familiarize yourself with federal discrimination laws and heed them

o Terminate employees (archived) properly to avoid discrimination claims

Recommended

o Create job descriptions (archived)

o Understand the definition of an "At Will" employee (archived)

o Have workers sign the proper Employment Agreement (archived)

o Protect the inventions employees create for you with an Employee Non-Compete and Confidentiality Agreement (archived)

o Give all new employees an Employee Handbook

o Lay out the employee benefits (archived) you will provide (paid leave and holidays, health insurance, bonuses)

o Choose a payroll service (ADP, Trinet, Paychex)

o Set up a system for employee discipline (archived)

.

Sales

Recommended

o Determine a pricing system for your product(s)

o Execute the 5 Step Sales Process

o Learn the art of persuasion (archived)

o Hire (archived) and train (archived) salespeople

o Use a sales management service (one that fits your business size, from smaller to larger companies)

o Pick a Customer Relationships Management System (archived) (CRM)

o Make a Customer Service Plan (archived) to standardize procedures for complaints and refunds

o Track which products perform better, and which have the best margins

o Decide on possible discounts, specials and coupons to attract new customers

o List your products on online marketplaces (archived)

.

CHECKLIST FOR YOUR SPECIFIC BUSINESS

.

Service-Based Businesses

Mandatory

o Determine the price (archived) of your services

Recommended

o Don’t forget to clarify the deliverable, deadline and cost in your service agreement

.

Retail/Brick and Mortar Businesses

Mandatory

o Check zoning laws

o Secure a lease

o Decide on days and hours of operation

o Determine staffing needs (for prime hours, slower times, closing an inventory)

o Secure the correct permits and licenses (archived) for your city and state

o Set up lighting and utilities

o Acquire furniture, register, racks, tables and wall displays, a register, etc.

o Get a state-specific Seller’s Permit or Resale Permit to properly collect sales tax

Recommended

o Choose a location with good foot traffic, size and appearance

o Speak with a commercial realtor to get an idea of what to look for and what to expect

o Establish agreements with your manufacturer, distributor or wholesale supplier

o Set up inventory tracking (understand a Stock Keeping Unit)

o Set up your Yelp page

.

Online Business

Mandatory

o Purchase computers

o Find images for your website

o Set up your e-commerce or online store (Shopify, FlyingCart (archived), Goodsie (archived))

o Choose a shipping or delivery system (FedEx, UPS, USPS)

o Set up a system for payment processing (PayPal, Google Checkout (archived))

Recommended

o Track and improve traffic through Google Analytics (archived)

o Determine storage options, such as cloud computing (Box, DropBox)

o Set up a system for IT bugs and tickets (Jira)

o If outsourced development is needed, use Guru or Elance
