TechVest’s Ultimate Business Plans - A Checklist for Big Ideas and Ventures

Ultimate Plan Components - The Importance of Substance

An ultimate Plan is not for the faint of heart nor for entrepreneurs that are unwilling to school themselves in the hard disciplines that transform a business plan into an operating plan and a tool for ongoing superiority in business planning and modeling quality. A Plan by itself does not buy you a working company or eager investors, but the discipline DOES buy you credibility. Merely showing an impressive plan is nothing more than packaging, it is the substance and the hard intelligence inside the plan that makes all the difference to all concerned. A plan should not be some fluff piece, but accurately reflect the well-thought-out knowledge and wisdom of all participants, and this is not a bunch of pretty sentences in a business plan template. In fact, any template is only a checklist against things potentially forgotten. There is no single template that fits multiple situations. Again, it is the substance behind the Plan that sells the situations. And that substance has to exude quality. The audience is investors and markets, internationally.

When investors look for IP, this does not just mean patents, but even moreso involves trade secrets and trade wisdom which tend to be unique and may not be so hard to copy or circumvent, but reflects the experience and personality and creativity of the participants. Any idea is a combination of intellectual capital, human capital, and only consequentially financial capital. Follow the money, because this is where a venture must and will succeed. Follow the money, but the money itself follows the elements of substance in any venture. Money will follow compellingly feasible operating plans.

Some of the components might seem less than substantive, but they too are there to prove their substance. There is actually a place for a certain degree of hype and self-marketing in business plans, but mainly to show how marketing savvy for the actual company and products will be powerfully and dramatically used to create a successful rate of return. If the right memorable mnemonics are discovered and woven into operating plans, investors and customers respond.

The combination of all of 25 components below (and there are more) creates the Ultimate Plan that represents the UPside in superior depth of any prospective venture or business that wants to accelerate. TechVest has been doing this for 25 years in every variety of venture, technology or non-technology. In fact, we maintain that the technology of planning is its own accelerant: what goes in can come out with optimal execution. … we do NOT generate grandiose "Corporate Strategy," we generate real-world “Strategic Tactics” that will have tangible returns.
