If you do not want to or cannot patent your idea and business, that is not abnormal. Trade Secrets and special knowledge account for most competitive advatage out in the real world. But you have all sorts of reasons to protect your idea and your business as much as possible from day one. Below are some helpful guidelines.

This checklist (by DocStoc and available downloadablehere) breaks down everything you must and should do when starting a business (archived).

Mandatory tasks are required under the law in order to operate a valid and compliant business.

Recommended tasks, although not strictly required under the law, are tasks that should be undertaken in order to grow and protect a new business.

Ideation & Protecting Ideas

Mandatory

o Pick a company name and check any potential trademark infringements onTrademarkia or KnowEm (archived)

o Determine whether your business name needs to be trademarked, and if does Trademark it at the USPTO

o Protect your written works and code through the Copyright Office

o If you are inventing or creating a product, understand patents and register for one at the USPTO

Recommended

o Vet your idea with the idea checklist

o Protect your ideas when sharing them with others by using an NDA

o Search your state’s corporation and LLC name registration database to ensure your business name has not been taken

o Check Google, Yellow Pages, and local directories to ensure your business name is not taken

o Learn how to select a good business name

.

Forming Your Entity

Mandatory

o Pick a structure (Sole Proprietorship (archived), Corporation (archived), S-Corp, LLC, Partnership, LP, LLP (archived), Non-Profit) and ensure that you comply with business formation requirements

o Creating a corporation? File Articles of Incorporation and Bylaws with the Secretary of State’s Office

o Forming an LLC? File the Articles of Organization and an Operating Agreement (archived) with the Secretary of State’s Office

o Get the necessary licenses and permits (archived) to operate legally

o Apply for an Employer Identification Number (EIN)

o If you are incorporating, appoint a Board of Directors (archived)

o For a corporation and LCC, you must register a service agent

Recommended

o Going into a Partnership? Create a Partnership Agreement (archived)

o Check your state governmentﾒs website for other state-specific registration, taxation and employment rules

o Get your DBA (archived) (Doing Business As) which allows a business to operate under a fictitious name (this is required for sole proprietorships that want to operate under any name other than the name of the business owner)

Open your company bank account

.

Identity and Branding

Recommended

o Design a logo (99designs)

o Search for and register a unique online domain name (GoDaddy, 1and1, Register)

o Create your website (Wordpress, Intuit, Weebly, Wix, SiteKreator)

o Ensure your business name is available on a wide variety of social networking websites in order to develop consistent branding (KnowEm (archived))

o Create and manage your social media (archived) presence

o Get business cards and other physical collateral (Vistaprint)

o List your business online with MerchantCircle, Manta, Yelp and DandB.com

.

Insurance

Mandatory

o Decide which types of business insurance (archived) you need.

  • Common: workers' compensation, unemployment (archived) and disability insurance
  • Others: property (archived), liability (archived) or business overhead insurance

o Check any potential insurance providers here
